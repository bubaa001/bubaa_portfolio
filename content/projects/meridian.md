## The idea
Serious organisations need a website that earns trust in seconds. Meridian uses editorial typography, restrained colour and cinematic photography to make any company look established.

## Highlights
- **Hero:** the headline reveals word by word over a full-bleed photo
- **Partner logos:** a scrolling marquee
- **About:** an editorial statement with animated key figures
- **Services:** hover to reveal details and imagery
- **Projects:** a showcase with grayscale-to-colour hover
- **Rest of the page:** leadership grid, insights list, enquiry form and offices

## Delivered as
A Next.js website plus an editable Figma design file.
