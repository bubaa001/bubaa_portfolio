## The idea
Shoe brands want to look like the big names, but their customers buy on the phone and pay with mobile money. STRYDE gives them both: a premium, animated storefront with ordering built for WhatsApp and M-Pesa.

## Highlights
- **Colourway hero:** the whole page changes colour with each colourway while the shoe floats and follows the mouse
- **Category list:** giant type, with product previews that follow the cursor
- **Product grid:** hover animations, badges and quick add with a size picker
- **Bag drawer:** quantities and totals in TZS, one-click **order on WhatsApp**
- **Drop countdown:** a live timer with a "notify me" signup
- **Original product photography** generated in Figma, with no third-party brands

## Delivered as
A Next.js website plus an editable Figma design file.
