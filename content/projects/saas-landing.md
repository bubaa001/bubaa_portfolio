## The brief
Concept project: a launch page for a fictional privacy-first analytics tool, built to show the kind of landing page I deliver for SaaS products, startups and service businesses.

## What's inside
- Hero with gradient headline, social proof and a live-looking product preview
- Logo strip, 6-feature grid with hover states, stats band
- **Monthly / yearly pricing toggle** with a highlighted plan
- Animated FAQ accordion and email-capture CTA
- Fully responsive, fast (static), SEO-ready metadata

## Good for
SaaS launches, paid-ad landing pages, waitlists, agencies and local businesses.
