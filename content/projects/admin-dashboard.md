## The brief
Concept project: the back-office of an e-commerce SaaS, showing the dashboards and internal tools I build for startups.

## What's inside
- Collapsible sidebar navigation with a mobile drawer
- KPI cards with month-over-month change
- Revenue area chart and traffic donut, hand-built in SVG (no heavy chart library)
- Orders table with **live search, status filters and column sorting**
- Responsive from phone to ultrawide

## In client builds
Connected to a real backend (Supabase, Firebase or a REST API) with login, roles and real data.
