## Description
Stop running your school from notebooks and spreadsheets. Get a **calm, modern dashboard** where the head of school, bursar and teachers see everything at a glance.

**Modules:**
📊 **Dashboard:** students, attendance today, fees collected, upcoming events, outstanding balances
👩‍🎓 **Students:** search, add, edit, filter by class, export to Excel/CSV
✅ **Attendance:** take the daily register in a few minutes (Present / Absent / Late)
📝 **Gradebook:** enter marks; averages, grades (A–F) and class positions calculate automatically
💰 **Fees:** see who has paid, record payments (M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, bank, cash)
🗓 **Timetable:** clear weekly timetable per class

Branded with your school's name, logo and colours. Works on laptop, tablet and phone.
